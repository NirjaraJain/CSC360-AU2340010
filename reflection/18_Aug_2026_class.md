### Drawing a Square

- Learned that a square has **four equal sides** and four right angles.
- The square can be defined using its **side length and position**.
- If the center of the square is `(cx, cy)` and the side length is `s`, the top-left corner can be calculated as:

```text
x = cx - s / 2
y = cy - s / 2
